About this app

Empower your children with real-world financial intelligence

Welcome to The Family Bank, the interactive banking app that turns money management, chores, and saving into an empowering educational experience for children.

Traditional piggy banks cannot demonstrate how modern finance and savings accounts work. The Family Bank bridges that gap by providing parents and children with a connected virtual banking platform that teaches deliberate spending, goal setting, and the power of compound interest.

Whether your child is saving for a personal milestone, earning an allowance through completed chores, or watching their savings grow with daily interest accrual, The Family Bank makes financial intelligence clear, tangible, and practical.

Designed for Families

The Family Bank supports multiple child profiles under a single family account. Each child receives a personalized balance, individual interest rate, and custom chore list tailored to their age and maturity.

🛡️ Safe & Educational: The Family Bank is a closed educational simulation. It does not connect to real bank accounts, require credit card links, or display third-party advertisements.

Key Features

Daily Accrued Compound Interest

Introduce the power of long-term savings early. Parents can assign a custom annual interest rate (APY) for each child. Every day, interest is automatically calculated and credited to the child's ledger, demonstrating the tangible benefits of saving rather than spending immediately.

Structured Chore Management & Allowances

Establish clear connections between effort and reward: parents configure chores with specific allowance amounts, children mark tasks completed with optional notes, and parents review and approve payouts through the centralized Approvals tab.

Goal Tracking & Savings Calculator

Help children develop delayed gratification habits with target milestones, visual progress meters showing exact remaining balances, and integrated forecasts showing how daily interest accelerates progress.

Purchase Request & Permission Workflow

Children submit purchase requests directly through their dashboard specifying item names, categories, and costs. Parents maintain full oversight by approving or declining requests before funds are deducted.

Secure Six-Digit Device Pairing

Children do not need email addresses or passwords. Parents generate a secure 6-digit pairing code from the parent portal to link their child's iPhone or iPad directly to their personal ledger.

Real-Time Family Cloud Synchronization

All balances, completed chores, approval requests, and interest payouts stay continuously synchronized across all family devices in real time.

Detailed Passbook Ledger

A clean, transparent digital bank statement providing a complete record of deposits, deductions, chore bonuses, and daily interest payouts.
