About this app

Take the headache out of coordination

Welcome to ScheduleAround, your all-in-one platform for sharing and booking resources with the people you know! Whether you are coordinating a shared study room, managing a community pool table, lending out expensive tools to neighbors, or scheduling times for the family car, ScheduleAround makes it effortless.

Say goodbye to messy group chats and double-bookings. With ScheduleAround, you can easily create items, invite friends, and let everyone book the time slots they need.

Perfect For:

  • Roommates & Families: Managing shared cars, TVs, or chores.
  • Apartment Complexes: Reserving pool tables, gym times, or BBQ grills.
  • Students: Booking study rooms or sharing project equipment.
  • Neighborhoods: Lending out lawnmowers, power tools, or camping gear.
  • Clubs & Teams: Coordinating practice spaces and shared inventory.

Stop stressing over scheduling conflicts. Download ScheduleAround today and experience the smartest way to share!

Key Features

Seamless Scheduling & Bookings

Easily browse available time slots for any shared item and book your spot in seconds. View your upcoming schedule in a beautiful calendar interface, and set up recurring bookings.

Custom Privacy & Approvals

You're always in control. Make your items Public for the community, Private for invite-only access, or Friends-Only. You can also require manual approval before anyone can book.

Quick QR Code Scanning

Every shared item gets its own unique QR code! Print it out and stick it on the item itself. Scan the code to instantly view the schedule and book a time.

Built-in Chat & Real-Time Notifications

Communicate directly with item owners or attendees. Get instant push notifications for approvals, cancellations, and real-time "I'm Here" arrival alerts.

Friends & Community Management

Add your neighbors, roommates, or classmates to your friends list. Easily manage who has access to your items.

Reviews & Feedback

Leave ratings and reviews for shared items to build trust and help your community know what to expect.
