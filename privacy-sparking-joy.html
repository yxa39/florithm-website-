Privacy Policy for Sparking Joy

Effective Date: September 2, 2026  ·  Contact: contact@florithm.com

Florithm LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, Sparking Joy ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use our App:

  • Photos & Camera Data: If you choose to use the AI Joy Scan feature, photos you take or select of your space and category piles are processed to analyze clutter and generate personalized tidying recommendations. Photos are processed strictly for generating your personalized tidying action plan and are not used to identify you personally.
  • Usage & Progress Data: We store your tidying progress, festival stages completed, streaks, milestones, experience points (XP), and preferences locally on your device or via secure cloud synchronization.
  • Device & Analytics Data: We may automatically collect standard diagnostic and crash data to ensure app stability and enhance user experience.
  • In-App Purchases: If you purchase a Pro Pass or premium subscription, payment transactions are processed directly by the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your credit card or financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the App and its mindful decluttering features.
  • Process AI Joy Scans and deliver tailored category-by-category tidying guidance.
  • Track your progress, milestones, and festival achievements.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve overall app reliability and performance.
  • Provide responsive customer support when requested.

3. Third-Party Services

We work with trusted third-party service providers to power specific app functionalities:

  • AI Processing Providers: Cloud-based AI APIs (such as Google Generative AI / Gemini or Apple Vision frameworks) used to analyze photos submitted during Joy Scans.
  • Apple App Store: For handling in-app billing, transactions, and subscription management.

4. Data Security & Retention

We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security safeguards to protect your data. Your photos and personal tidying notes are treated with respect and confidentiality. You can reset or delete your tidying data anytime directly within the App's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:
