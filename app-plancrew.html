About this app

Chat, plan, and stay in sync

PlanCrew brings messaging and planning together, so your group can spend less time coordinating and more time together.

Chat one-on-one or create groups for friends, trips, clubs, teams, and everyday plans. Share messages, photos, voice notes, emojis, and GIFs—all while keeping schedules and event details close to the conversation.

Perfect For:

  • Friend Groups: Planning nights out, weekend getaways, and spontaneous hangouts.
  • Travel Crews: Coordinating trips with shared itineraries, checklists, and calendars.
  • Clubs & Teams: Managing practice schedules, meetings, and team events.
  • Roommates & Families: Keeping up with household plans and shared responsibilities.
  • Anyone: Who wants one place to chat, plan, and stay in sync with their crew.

Whether you're planning a night out, coordinating a trip, managing a club, or simply keeping up with friends, PlanCrew gives your crew one place to chat, plan, and stay in sync.

Key Features

Plan Together

Create events in shared chat calendars, add locations and notes, build checklists, invite attendees, and organize recurring plans.

Stay Connected

Send real-time direct and group messages. Reply in threads, share media, react with emojis, and keep every conversation organized.

Build Your Crew

Find friends, create groups, invite members, personalize your profile, and organize chats into folders.

Share the Moment

Post photos, explore group galleries, and save collections you want to revisit.

PlanCrew Plus

Messaging, groups, emojis, photos, voice messages, and basic calendar features remain free. PlanCrew Plus adds voice-to-text, unlimited GIFs, extra customization, more chat folders, and an optional supporter badge.
