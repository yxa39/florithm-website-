About this app

Create a peaceful, clutter-free living environment

Transform your home and clear your mind with Sparking Joy, the mindful decluttering and home organization companion designed to help you create a peaceful, clutter-free living environment.

Say goodbye to messy rooms and tidying burnout. Following the world-renowned category-by-category method, Sparking Joy guides you step-by-step through discarding what no longer serves you and organizing what brings true value to your life.

The 5-Stage Tidying Festival

Declutter by category rather than room to permanently prevent clutter rebounds:

  • Stage 1: Clothing — Master vertical folding and curate your ideal wardrobe.
  • Stage 2: Books — Keep only the titles that inspire and nourish your life.
  • Stage 3: Papers — Simplify bills, warranties, and records with clear retention rules.
  • Stage 4: Komono (Miscellaneous) — Tame electronics, kitchenware, cosmetics, and hobby gear.
  • Stage 5: Sentimental — Mindfully honor cherished memories, photos, and heirlooms.

Perfect For:

  • Anyone feeling overwhelmed by clutter or disorganized spaces
  • Enthusiasts of mindful living, minimalism, and intentional design
  • Moving into a new home, spring cleaning, or seasonal closet resets
  • Anyone seeking calm, focus, and a sustainable home routine

Start your Tidying Festival today with Sparking Joy and surround yourself only with what you love.

Key Features

AI Joy Scan and Photo Assessment

Snap a photo of your space before you start. The intelligent AI analyzes your clutter, verifies your category pile, and crafts a personalized action plan tailored to your room.

The 5-Stage Tidying Festival

Tidy systematically through Clothing, Books, Papers, Komono, and Sentimental items to build decision-making sensitivity and prevent rebound clutter forever.

Step-by-Step Folding & Organizing Guides

Learn self-standing vertical folding techniques and drawer-organization principles so every item remains visible and easy to retrieve.

Mindful Focus Timer & Calming Audio

Set focused 15 to 45-minute decluttering sprints with soothing ambient soundscapes and mindful chimes to keep you in a calm, productive flow.

Before & After Transformation Showcase

Compare your progress with an interactive Before and After slider and generate clean transformation recap cards to celebrate your results.

Gamified Progress & Milestones

Earn experience points, unlock mindful achievements, track your streak, and watch your level advance as you progress through your home festival.

Ideal Lifestyle Vision

Define the lifestyle you aspire to live and align your home environment with your personal peace, focus, and joy.
