Privacy Policy for Read with Booki

Effective Date: May 26, 2026

Florithm LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use our App:

  • Usage Data: We may automatically collect certain information when you access or use the App. This may include your device type, operating system, and information about your interactions with the App.
  • In-App Purchases: If you choose to make in-app purchases, your payment information will be processed by third-party payment processors (such as Apple App Store). We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • App Data: Some data related to your reading progress, vocabulary, and preferences may be stored locally on your device or synced with our servers to provide a seamless experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our App.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our App's features and user experience.
  • Process transactions related to in-app purchases.
  • Respond to customer service requests and provide support.
  • Analyze usage trends and improve the App's performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with third-party service providers that perform services for us, such as hosting, data analysis, customer service, and payment processing. Specifically, we use:

  • RevenueCat: For managing in-app purchases and subscriptions.
  • AI Services: For providing AI-driven features, such as word explanations and interactive reading assistants.

These third-party service providers have their own privacy policies addressing how they use such information.

4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your information. However, please be aware that no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other types of misuse.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take the necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this Privacy Policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
