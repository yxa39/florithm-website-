Privacy Policy for ScheduleAround

Effective Date: July 16, 2026

Florithm LLC ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways when you use our App:

  • Usage Data: We may automatically collect certain information when you access or use the App. This may include your device type, operating system, and information about your interactions with the App.
  • In-App Purchases: If you choose to make in-app purchases, your payment information will be processed by third-party payment processors (such as Apple App Store). We do not collect or store your payment card details.
  • App Data: Some data related to your schedules, items, and preferences may be stored locally on your device or synced with our servers to provide a seamless experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain our App.
  • Improve, personalize, and expand our App's features and user experience.
  • Process transactions related to in-app purchases.
  • Respond to customer service requests and provide support.
  • Analyze usage trends and improve the App's performance.

3. Third-Party Services

We may share your information with third-party service providers that perform services for us, such as hosting, data analysis, customer service, and payment processing. Specifically, we use Firebase and Google Cloud for our backend infrastructure and database storage.

4. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the personal information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable.
