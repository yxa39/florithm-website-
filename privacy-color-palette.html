Palette — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026  ·  Last Updated: November 6, 2026  ·  Contact: contact@florithm.com

Florithm LLC ("Florithm", "we", "us", or "our") operates Palette (the "App", "Service", or "Application"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy rights.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use Palette, and outlines your legal rights regarding that data.

By installing, accessing, or using Palette, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide:

  • Account Credentials: When you sign up via Apple ID, Google Sign-In, or Email, we collect your unique account identifier, display name, handle, email address, and profile picture.
  • Wardrobe & Closet Photos: Images of personal clothing garments, shoes, accessories, and outfit look compositions you upload to your digital closet.
  • Diagnostic Portraits & Selfies: Selfies or portraits captured or uploaded for Seasonal Color Analysis (12-Season and 16-shade harmony wheel).
  • AI Stylist Consultations: Chat messages, styling prompts, and look preferences exchanged with our virtual AI stylists.
  • Community Interactions: Publicly shared looks, comments, likes, saves, and following connections.
  • Support Inquiries: Communications sent to contact@florithm.com.

1.2 Automatically Collected Information:

  • Device & Technical Data: Device model, operating system version (iOS/iPadOS), app version, unique device tokens, language settings, and crash diagnostics.
  • Usage Data: In-app navigation, feature interactions, color selections, and search queries.
  • Approximate Climate/Weather: If requested for climate-adaptive outfit suggestions, approximate weather data (e.g. temperature and general conditions) is retrieved. We do not track or store continuous, precise GPS location.

1.3 Purchase & Financial Data:

All subscription purchases (Palette Pro) are processed securely by Apple Inc. through the Apple App Store. Florithm LLC never collects, processes, or stores your credit card numbers or payment details. We receive only transaction confirmation tokens from Apple StoreKit.

2. Facial & Diagnostic Image Privacy Disclosures

Because Palette provides personal color diagnostics using portrait photos, we uphold strict privacy standards:

  1. Purpose-Limited Analysis: Portrait photos are analyzed solely to determine color undertones, contrast, value, and seasonal color harmonies (e.g., Deep Winter, Soft Autumn, Light Spring, Cool Summer) and to display your portrait inside your interactive 16-color harmony wheel.
  2. No Biometric Surveillance: Palette DOES NOT use facial recognition technology to identify individuals or construct biometric surveillance profiles.
  3. No Selling of Photos: We NEVER sell, license, or trade your facial images, portraits, or wardrobe photos to third-party data brokers, surveillance firms, or commercial advertisers.
  4. User Controlled: You can retake or replace your diagnostic photo at any time directly in the Season tab, which permanently overwrites the previous diagnostic image.
  5. AI Data Protection: Outfit and wardrobe images analyzed by AI styling endpoints are processed securely and are never used to train public machine learning models on your likeness.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information strictly to:

  • Deliver personal color analysis and interactive shade harmony wheels.
  • Provide real-time AI styling advice and wardrobe curation.
  • Organize your private digital closet and outfit formulas.
  • Manage social community features (for outfits you choose to share publicly).
  • Maintain app security, prevent fraud, and authenticate accounts.
  • Improve app performance and respond to customer support requests.

4. How Information Is Shared

Florithm LLC does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with:

  • Cloud & Infrastructure Providers: Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore cloud database, encrypted Cloud Storage, Realtime Database) operating under enterprise security and confidentiality agreements.
  • AI Model Infrastructure: Google Cloud Vertex / Gemini API endpoints for generative styling consultations, operating under enterprise terms prohibiting customer data usage for foundation model training.
  • Apple Inc.: For secure App Store payment verification.
  • Legal & Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or to protect the safety, rights, and security of Florithm LLC and its users.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of company assets, where the successor entity remains bound by this Privacy Policy.

5. Data Security & Storage

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transfers between the App and servers are secured using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Uploaded images and database records are stored with AES-256 bit encryption in Google Cloud / Firebase facilities.
  • Access Restrictions: Production data access is strictly limited to authorized engineering personnel using multi-factor authentication.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion

6.1 Retention: We retain your account data and wardrobe items as long as your account remains active.

6.2 Instant Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time:

In-App Path: Profile (My Looks) > Settings (Gear) > Account & Security > Delete Account.

Upon confirmation:

  • Your user account, profile record, and email authentication are deleted.
  • All wardrobe photos, look books, comments, and diagnostic selfies are permanently removed from cloud storage.
  • AI stylist chat logs are permanently cleared.

You may also request account deletion by emailing contact@florithm.com. Requests are completed within thirty (30) days.

7. Your Legal Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may hold specific legal rights:

  • European Economic Area (EEA) / UK (GDPR): Rights to access, rectify, erase ("right to be forgotten"), restrict processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent.
  • California (CCPA / CPRA): Rights to know what personal information is collected, request deletion, request correction, and freedom from discriminatory treatment. Florithm LLC does not sell personal information or share personal data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

To exercise your privacy rights, email contact@florithm.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Palette is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA/UK). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact contact@florithm.com and we will promptly delete it.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When updates occur, we will revise the "Last Updated" date and provide notice within the App or via email before changes take effect.
