About this app

Do you think you are the main character?

Do you practice your Oscar acceptance speech in the shower? Or are you just a humble little potato navigating a chaotic world?

Find out the truth with "Am I a Narcissist?", a hilarious and completely unscientific personality quiz designed to test your ego! Answer 20 funny, introspective questions to discover exactly where you land on the vanity spectrum.

Disclaimer: If you were a true narcissist, you probably wouldn't even download this app! This test is intended purely for entertainment and laughs, and is not a clinical or medical diagnostic tool.

Key Features

20 Relatable Questions

Hilariously relatable questions about your ego, social habits, and daily reflections.

4 Unique Result Levels

Find out if you are ranging from a "Humble Potato" to "Your Majesty".

Beautiful Illustrations

Beautifully illustrated, funny cartoon characters for every result.

Perfect Party Game

The perfect party game or icebreaker to play with friends!
